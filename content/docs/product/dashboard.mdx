The dashboard is the operating center for AnswerLoops. Its grouped navigation keeps monitoring, support work, knowledge improvement, and workspace configuration distinct while preserving quick access to every channel.

## Stat cards

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Auto-Answered | Questions resolved automatically by AI |
| Deflection rate | Deflected / Total — your key efficiency metric |
| Open | Questions that still need attention |
| In Progress | Questions currently being handled |
| Resolved | Completed support conversations |
| Needs Review | Answers waiting for a human decision |
| AI Drafts Pending | Draft replies ready to review |
| SLA Breaches | Tickets past their configured response target |

## Deflection rate

A deflection happens when the AI answers a question with confidence above your threshold (default 0.8) and the user doesn't escalate to a human. Higher = less manual support work.

Tickets classified as `bug` are excluded from this metric entirely — the knowledge base can't contain a fix that doesn't exist yet, so declining to auto-answer a bug report is correct behaviour, not a miss. Deflection rate reflects how well AnswerLoops handles questions the knowledge base can actually answer.

## Quick actions

The dashboard surfaces quick links to the most common tasks: adding knowledge, reviewing deflection trends, configuring AI behavior, and reviewing the generated FAQ. The open-ticket panel prioritizes unresolved work, while the SLA panel separates time-sensitive conversations from the rest of the queue.

## Live updates

The dashboard updates itself as work comes in — a new ticket from any
channel, a status change, an AI draft becoming ready, a teammate accepting an
invite — without a manual refresh and without polling on a timer.

It holds one Server-Sent Events connection to the app while a dashboard tab
is open and focused — one per tab, shared by every part of the page that
listens for changes, including the live team-member list on Settings. A
database trigger notifies that connection when a ticket changes or a
notification is raised or cleared for your workspace, and the page re-fetches
once in response. (Marking a notification read is not pushed to other open
tabs — their unread count catches up on the next refresh.) When nothing is
changing, nothing is requested. The connection is dropped while the tab is in
the background and re-established when you return to it.

If that connection is interrupted (a proxy timeout, a database failover, a
laptop waking from sleep), the page reconnects on its own and re-fetches
immediately, since a change may have landed while it was down. While a tab is
focused, a background refresh every 60 seconds acts as a safety net, so a
missed event surfaces within a minute; a backgrounded tab has no timer and
instead refreshes the moment you switch back to it. The connection is also
recycled roughly every 15 minutes on its own — the page rebuilds it with no
visible interruption — so a connection that has gone stale without signalling
it cannot linger.

On a pooled Postgres connection (Neon's `-pooler` endpoint, PgBouncer,
Supabase's pooled port), set `DIRECT_DATABASE_URL` to the non-pooled
connection string — the same requirement as bot config hot-reload. Without
it, live updates fall back to the 60-second safety refresh.

## Navigation

The workspace sidebar groups pages by the job they support:

- **Monitor:** Dashboard, Analytics, and Simulation
- **Support:** Tickets and widget Leads
- **Improve:** Knowledge Base, FAQ, and Knowledge Gaps
- **Configure:** Billing and Settings

On mobile, the same navigation is available from the menu button in the top bar.
